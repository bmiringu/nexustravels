Cancellation and booking policy

Written down in advance, so there is nothing to argue about afterwards.

Free cancellation

Cancel more than 12 hours before pickup and there is no charge, for any reason at all.

Inside 12 hours

Half the fare. The vehicle is already held for you by then and we have turned other work away.

If nobody appears

Where we cannot reach you within 60 minutes of the agreed time, the full fare applies. We will have called and messaged first.

A late flight is never charged

We track your flight from the moment it leaves. If it lands late your pickup moves with it, at no cost, however long the delay.

Waiting time

Sixty minutes free at the airport, thirty minutes anywhere else. After that it is KSh 500 per half hour.

The quoted price is the price

Before you book

What happens if my flight is delayed?

Nothing, from your side. We track your flight from departure and adjust the pickup automatically. There is no charge for a delay caused by the airline, and the first sixty minutes of waiting at the airport is free in any case.

How will I find the driver at arrivals?

He will be standing at the arrivals door with a board carrying your name. You will already have his photograph, name and the vehicle registration on your phone, sent two hours before pickup.

Is the price per person or per vehicle?

Per vehicle. Three people travelling to the CBD in a sedan pay the sedan rate once, not three times. Choose the vehicle by passengers and luggage.

How do I pay?

M-Pesa or card after the journey. Corporate account holders are invoiced monthly on 30-day terms with a compliant tax invoice.

Do you charge more at night or for waiting?

A 25% surcharge applies between 22:00 and 05:00, and it is included in any price we quote you — never added afterwards. At the airport the first sixty minutes of waiting is free; after that it is KSh 500 per half hour.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel more than two hours before pickup and there is no charge. Inside two hours it is half the fare, and a no-show without notice is the full fare. If your airline delays or cancels the flight, there is no charge at all.

Can you carry a large group?

Up to seven passengers in one executive van. Beyond that we run more than one vehicle together, or bring in a coach for a conference or a delegation.

Do you provide child seats?

Yes, at no extra charge, but we need to know in advance — tell us the child's age when you book so we bring the right one.

Can you drive me for a whole day of meetings?

Yes — that is our car hire with driver service. Half day is up to four hours and 60 km; full day is up to ten hours and 150 km. The car and driver stay with you throughout.

Do you go into the national parks?

We handle the road transfer to and from the park gates. Carrying guests commercially inside the parks requires separate tourist service vehicle licensing, so for game drives we connect you with a licensed operator we trust.
